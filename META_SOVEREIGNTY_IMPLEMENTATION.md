# Meta-Sovereignty Analysis Implementation

## Overview

This implementation adds sophisticated theoretical analysis to the AI debate system, incorporating three complementary analytical frameworks:

1. **Ezrahi's Political Fiction Analysis** - Identifies necessary political fictions in AI governance discourse
2. **Fearon's Costly Signal Analysis** - Distinguishes genuine commitments from cheap talk
3. **Meta-Sovereignty Synthesis** - Analyzes how states perform sovereignty through signals and imaginaries

## Key Changes

### 1. Removed Hardcoded Content
- Eliminated all static fallback content
- Removed the "adjusts spectacles thoughtfully" phrase
- All analysis now dynamically generated by LLM agents

### 2. Three-Layer Analysis Architecture

#### Layer 1: Ezrahi's Political Fictions
The LLM is positioned as Yaron Ezrahi to analyze:
- Political fictions in diplomatic statements (e.g., "democratic AI governance")
- How search results reveal contradictions to these fictions
- The performative nature of AI governance discourse

#### Layer 2: Fearon's Costly Signals
The LLM is positioned as James Fearon to identify:
- Costly signals in search results (actual investments, regulations)
- Cheap talk in diplomatic statements
- The credibility gap between rhetoric and action

#### Layer 3: Meta-Sovereignty Analysis
Synthesizes both analyses through the thesis lens:
- Infrastructural imaginaries (projected AI futures)
- Performance of control vs actual control
- The entanglement paradox of AI sovereignty

## Theoretical Framework

### Meta-Sovereignty Thesis
"The emerging AI Cold War reveals a paradox: the harder states strive to territorialize and domesticate AI infrastructures, the more they expose their reliance on globally entangled systems of chips, cloud, and compute that no actor fully commands."

### Key Concepts
- **Infrastructural Imaginaries**: Projected AI futures that claim authority
- **Performance of Control**: Sovereignty enacted through gestures rather than possession
- **Entanglement Paradox**: Attempts to domesticate AI reveal deeper dependencies
